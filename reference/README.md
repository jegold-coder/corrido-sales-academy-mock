# Reference material from the Tequila Corrido CMO

These are the links the CMO shared with our team, kept in one place to help with changes to the website and the game. This folder holds links only. The documents, decks and videos stay where they are, and some of them may need a sign-in or access from the owner.

The CMO has not sent any sales material yet (real customers, objections, pitches, pricing talk). Until it arrives, the missions and lessons use public facts and placeholders. When it does arrive, it goes into the facts editor (Dashboard > Settings) and the scenarios.
